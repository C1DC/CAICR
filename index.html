Do the hands-on labs (in your terminal)

Open a terminal in the lab folder and run:

# see every module: lesson, lab and deliverable ./labctl learn # read a module, then do it for real on isolated targets ./labctl learn m3 ./labctl start m3 # bring up the safe practice targets ./labctl verify m3 # submit your findings, get graded # get a module's deliverable template to fill in ./labctl worksheet m2 # track how far you've come ./labctl progress
Safety

Every lab runs against isolated targets on your own machine (addresses like 10.13.x.x). Only ever scan or attack those. Using these tools against any other system is against the law (Ghana Cybersecurity Act 2020, Act 1038).
