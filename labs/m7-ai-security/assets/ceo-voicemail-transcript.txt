ANALYST NOTES (what to teach the student)
-----------------------------------------
* The voice is a near-perfect clone of the MD (AI voice cloning needs only a few
  seconds of sample audio — plenty exists in public speeches and webinars).
* Deepfake DETECTORS are unreliable (high error rates; attackers adapt), so the
  decision must NOT rest on "it sounded real."
* The RED FLAGS here are behavioural, not acoustic:
    - extreme urgency and a deadline ("today, before 12")
    - an explicit instruction NOT to call back ("don't call back")
    - a demand to BYPASS the normal approvers ("don't loop in the usual approvers")
    - secrecy ("strictly confidential")
    - a request arriving over an out-of-band channel (a WhatsApp voice note)
* The CONTROL that defeats this every time: independent, out-of-band
  verification — call the MD back on a PRE-ESTABLISHED known number (or verify in
  person), and enforce dual-control on any payment regardless of who "asked".
